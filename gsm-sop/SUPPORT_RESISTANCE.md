# GSM Support & Resistance SOP

## Core principle

Support & Resistance are **zones, not single lines**.

- Support: area where price previously reacted upward.
- Resistance: area where price previously reacted downward.

The GSM material frames the area as a region where buyers and sellers repeatedly react, not a single exact number.

## Rule 01 — Draw the Zone

- Two touches are enough to identify/draw an S/R zone.
- Three touches strengthen/confirm the zone.
- The next test can either hold or break; therefore repeated tests increase importance but do not guarantee another bounce.

Automation implication: count meaningful touches, but never convert touch count into certainty.

## Rule 02 — Role Reversal

When a level breaks and price later returns:

- Broken Resistance can become Support.
- Broken Support can become Resistance.

The role switch is confirmed by the market reaction after the break/retest, not by the break alone.

## Rule 03 — Major vs Minor

- Zones tested more often / showing stronger reactions are treated as Major.
- Zones with fewer/weaker reactions are Minor.
- Priority should generally be given to stronger/major zones rather than cluttering the chart with every minor level.

## Rule 04 — Good Zone vs Bad Zone

Good zone characteristics:
- Clear reaction when price reaches the area.
- Rejection/bounce is visible and decisive.

Bad zone characteristics:
- Price repeatedly weaves/chops through the area.
- No clear reaction boundary.

Automation implication: zone quality should measure reaction clarity, not only number of touches.

## Rule 05 — False Breakout

A temporary move through a zone that quickly returns back can be a false breakout rather than a true structural break.

Codex must distinguish:
- true break/acceptance beyond the zone;
- wick/temporary excursion;
- false break followed by rejection back through the area.

## Rule 06 — Wait for the Zone

The GSM material emphasizes waiting for price to reach the planned S/R area before executing.

Do not chase price in the middle of the chart simply because direction looks attractive.

## Coding constraints

- Use zones, not exact one-price lines.
- Keep touch count and reaction quality separate.
- Support/Resistance role-flip state must be explicit.
- Major/Minor is a hierarchy/quality concept, not an excuse to ignore a nearer valid zone without a tested rule.
- False-break logic must be based on price behavior around the zone.
- External GitHub support/resistance algorithms are Candidate ideas only; they cannot redefine this GSM source behavior without explicit user approval.
